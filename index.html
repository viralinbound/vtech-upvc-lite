FAQ

Everything you wanted to ask about uPVC

How long does the entire process take?+

VTech states the typical timeline from first site visit to final installation is 18–21 days, covering measurement, design approval, custom manufacturing in its Bengaluru factory, and installation.

Are uPVC windows costlier than aluminium?+

VTech says uPVC costs about 10–20% more than aluminium on day one, but lower energy bills, zero maintenance and a longer lifespan make it cheaper over 15 years.

What warranty do you provide?+

windows.vtechwindows.com lists a 20-year manufacturer warranty on the uPVC profile, hardware, gaskets and reinforcement; glass and mosquito mesh are not covered. Please confirm the exact terms with VTech when you get your quote.

Can I see the products before deciding?+

Yes. Visit the showroom at M.N.S Arcade, Dr. Rajkumar Road, 6th Block, Rajajinagar, Bangalore. VTech also offers tours of its manufacturing facility at Kalenahalli.

Do you remove the old windows?+

Yes. VTech’s installation team removes existing windows, clears the debris and installs the new ones, with no separate contractor needed.

Which areas do you serve?+

All of Bangalore, including Whitefield, Sarjapur, Electronic City, HSR, Indiranagar, Hebbal, Jayanagar and Rajajinagar, plus larger projects across Karnataka and India.

*Warranty and figures as stated on vtechwindows.com and windows.vtechwindows.com. Please confirm terms with VTech.
